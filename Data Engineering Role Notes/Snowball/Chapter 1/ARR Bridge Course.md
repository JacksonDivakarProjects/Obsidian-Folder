A structured, ten-lesson beginner course on the ARR Snowball / ARR Bridge concept — start here if you're new to the topic, then use the [[Snowball|Snowball hub]]'s deeper reference notes once you're comfortable with the fundamentals.

Every lesson uses the same running example — a small fictional SaaS company called **Nimbus Cloud Storage** — so the numbers and customers carry through from Lesson 1 to Lesson 10 instead of resetting each time.

## The course

1. [[Lesson 1 - Why ARR Needs a Bridge|Lesson 1 — Why ARR Needs a Bridge]] — why a single ARR number hides completely different underlying stories.
2. [[Lesson 2 - The Bridge Vocabulary|Lesson 2 — The Bridge Vocabulary]] — precise definitions of New, Expansion, Contraction, Churn, and Reactivation.
3. [[Lesson 3 - NRR and GRR, the Headline Metrics|Lesson 3 — NRR and GRR, the Headline Metrics]] — the two summary numbers investors and operators actually watch, with real 2026 benchmarks.
4. [[Lesson 4 - Logo Churn vs Revenue Churn|Lesson 4 — Logo Churn vs Revenue Churn]] — why counting accounts and counting dollars can tell very different stories.
5. [[Lesson 5 - The 5-Step Build Process|Lesson 5 — The 5-Step Build Process]] — the mechanical process for turning raw subscription data into a bridge.
6. [[Lesson 6 - Getting Real-World Dates Right|Lesson 6 — Getting Real-World Dates Right]] — a decision guide for whatever shape your raw data actually comes in.
7. [[Lesson 7 - Writing Your First Snowball Query|Lesson 7 — Writing Your First Snowball Query]] — a real, runnable SQL query explained CTE by CTE.
8. [[Lesson 8 - Going Multi-Grain|Lesson 8 — Going Multi-Grain]] — why production systems need customer → product → service grain, not just customer grain.
9. [[Lesson 9 - Choosing Your Implementation|Lesson 9 — Choosing Your Implementation]] — imperative T-SQL procedure vs. portable declarative CTE chain.
10. [[Lesson 10 - Validating, Visualizing, and Avoiding Mistakes|Lesson 10 — Validating, Visualizing, and Avoiding Mistakes]] — the tie-out check, waterfall charts, and a pitfalls checklist.

## Ready for more?

Once you've finished all ten lessons here, continue to [[ARR Bridge Course - Chapter 2|Chapter 2]] — a harder, more advanced continuation that pushes into cohort analysis, YTD mechanics, non-standard ARR, and full production data-engineering practice (incremental builds, automated testing, orchestration, and a capstone system-design exercise).

## How this course relates to the reference notes

This course teaches the same material as the [[Snowball|Snowball hub]]'s reference notes, but as a guided walkthrough with one continuous worked example, self-check questions, and explicit beginner scaffolding. Each lesson links to its corresponding reference note for the complete technical detail — treat the lessons as the on-ramp and the reference notes as what you come back to later when implementing this for real.
