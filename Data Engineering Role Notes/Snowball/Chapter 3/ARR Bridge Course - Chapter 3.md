The applied continuation of [[ARR Bridge Course - Chapter 2|Chapter 2]]. Chapters 1 and 2 built the conceptual and production-engineering foundation on a clean, fully-labeled example (Nimbus Cloud Storage). Chapter 3 closes the last gap: what to do when someone hands you their *actual* data — unlabeled, inconsistent, with no lesson title warning you where the traps are — and expects a correct, delivered ARR bridge back.

Five lessons, building toward one hands-on capstone using a new running example, **Corvid Systems**, whose raw exports you profile in Lesson 1, scope in Lesson 2, and fully resolve in Lesson 5.

## The course

1. [[Lesson 1 - Profiling a Stranger's Dataset|Lesson 1 — Profiling a Stranger's Dataset]] — a reusable 9-point checklist for interrogating an unfamiliar subscription/billing export before writing any bridge SQL.
2. [[Lesson 2 - Scoping the Ask|Lesson 2 — Scoping the Ask]] — turning profiling flags into stakeholder questions, with a worked scoping conversation and a reusable checklist.
3. [[Lesson 3 - Building the Delivery Artifact|Lesson 3 — Building the Delivery Artifact]] — actually constructing a waterfall chart (the universal invisible-base-bar technique) and an executive one-pager.
4. [[Lesson 4 - Documentation and Handoff|Lesson 4 — Documentation and Handoff]] — an 8-section methodology doc template that makes every judgment call reviewable.
5. [[Lesson 5 - Capstone, Ship It|Lesson 5 — Capstone, Ship It]] — hands-on: six real data traps in Corvid Systems' raw exports, resolved end to end, with a verified final bridge.

## Why this chapter exists

Chapters 1 and 2 taught you to build a bridge and to run it in production. Neither one taught you what happens in the ninety minutes before you write your first query — reading a dataset nobody has explained to you, and negotiating with a stakeholder who hasn't yet told you what they actually need. That gap is most of the real difficulty in "someone hands me their data and I have to deliver a snowball." This chapter closes it.
